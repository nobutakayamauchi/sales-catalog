SALES DISTRIBUTION NETWORK / SDN

Turn any Stripe checkout into an affiliate sales network.

Commissions, payouts, refunds, and reversals included.

SDN is a bounded sales-distribution layer for Stripe sellers: route buyers through referral links or tokens, attribute the sale, calculate commission, and keep settlement aligned when money moves forward or backward.

THE IDEA

Your checkout should not be the end of the sales system.

A product can already accept payment and still be hard to distribute. SDN adds the bounded layer around the checkout that turns referrals into attributable sales and attributable sales into settlement.

Referral attribution

Give a referrer a link or token and preserve who brought the buyer into the Stripe checkout flow.

Commission + payout

Turn an eligible referred sale into a commission record and carry it through the payout lifecycle.

Refund + reversal

When the underlying payment is refunded, keep the affiliate-side settlement state aligned instead of leaving stale commission behind.

ONE SALES FLOW

FOR SELLERS

Already selling with Stripe?

SDN is aimed at sellers who want other people to distribute their product without rebuilding referral attribution, commission state, payout state, and refund corrections as separate systems.

BOUNDARY FIRST

Build the real chain first.

V1 is focused on one product, affiliate distribution, and real settlement. Broader product-owner and network-to-network capabilities stay outside V1 until real demand requires them.
